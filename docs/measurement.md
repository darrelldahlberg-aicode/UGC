# UGC Measurement and Creator Decisions

## Core Rule

Measure against the campaign objective. Do not reward or penalize a creator for metrics that were not part of the campaign's job.

Missing metrics stay `null` or are listed as unknown. They must not be converted to zero solely to make a record complete.

## Objective-to-Metric Mapping

### Awareness

Primary signals may include:

- reach
- views
- hook rate
- hold rate
- average watch time
- completion rate

### Engagement

Primary signals may include:

- likes
- comments
- shares
- saves
- watch quality

### Traffic

Primary signals may include:

- CTR
- click evidence when available
- landing-page behavior from the owning analytics system

### Leads

Primary signals may include:

- leads
- cost per lead
- conversion quality when available

### Sales

Primary signals may include:

- purchases
- revenue
- cost per acquisition
- ROAS

### Affiliate

Primary signals may include:

- attributable affiliate revenue
- purchases or conversions available from the affiliate source

## Evidence Sources

Every performance record must identify the evidence sources used. If a platform, affiliate dashboard, media platform, CRM, or analytics source does not provide a metric, keep it unknown.

## Creator Scorecard

Separate:

1. pre-campaign qualification
2. in-campaign operating performance
3. content quality and approval performance
4. audience and business outcomes
5. cost efficiency
6. reusable learning

The final creator decision must be exactly one of:

- `renew`
- `retain_specific_use`
- `retest_new_variable`
- `pause`
- `retire`

A decision should cite the campaign evidence and reusable learning behind it.
