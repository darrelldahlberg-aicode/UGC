# Creator Outreach and Response Tracking

## Purpose

Contact qualified creators, gather missing commercial and rights information, and preserve the response trail without promising unapproved terms.

## Outreach Sequence

### Initial Outreach

Include:

- who is contacting the creator
- why the creator appears relevant
- campaign category or brand context when approved for disclosure
- requested deliverable type
- expected timing
- whether the opportunity involves creator posting, brand-owned UGC, paid usage, or a mix
- one clear next action

### Follow-Up

If there is no response, send a short follow-up that references the original opportunity and asks whether the creator wants details or prefers to pass.

### Qualification Questions

Ask only what is still missing, such as:

- availability
- turnaround time
- relevant category experience
- sample work
- audience information
- conflicts or exclusivity
- preferred communication channel

Creator answers remain `creator_provided` unless independently verified.

### Rate Request

Ask for:

- base deliverable rate
- revision terms
- payment terms
- add-on rates if separate rights or services carry added cost

Do not turn one creator's quoted rate into a market standard.

### Rights Clarification

Ask separately about:

- creator-posted organic rights
- brand usage rights
- paid media rights
- whitelisting or partnership-ad access
- allowed channels
- start and end dates
- renewal terms
- exclusivity terms

Agreement to create content does not imply paid-media usage rights.

### Decline or Closeout

Close the conversation respectfully when the creator is not selected, unavailable, outside approved terms, or no longer a fit. Record the reason internally without inventing negative conclusions.

## Response Tracking

Track:

- outreach date
- outreach channel
- current response status
- response date
- requested rate
- stated availability
- rights position
- unanswered questions
- follow-up date
- next action

Suggested response states:

- `not_contacted`
- `contacted`
- `follow_up_sent`
- `responded`
- `interested`
- `not_interested`
- `needs_more_information`
- `selected`
- `closed`
