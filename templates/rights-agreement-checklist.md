# Rights Agreement Checklist

## Commercial Scope

- [ ] Deliverables are defined
- [ ] Revision terms are defined
- [ ] Rate is recorded
- [ ] Payment terms are recorded

## Rights

- [ ] Creator-posted organic rights are explicitly recorded
- [ ] Brand usage rights are explicitly recorded
- [ ] Paid media rights are explicitly recorded
- [ ] Whitelisting or partnership-ad access is explicitly recorded
- [ ] Allowed channels are recorded when limited
- [ ] Rights start date is recorded when a dated grant applies
- [ ] Rights end date is recorded when a dated grant applies
- [ ] Renewal terms are recorded
- [ ] Exclusivity terms are recorded or marked not required

## Compliance

- [ ] Required disclosures are recorded
- [ ] Prohibited claims are recorded
- [ ] Known conflicts are resolved or documented

## Approvals

- [ ] Strategy approval state is recorded
- [ ] Creative approval state is recorded
- [ ] Brand approval state is recorded
- [ ] Quality approval state is recorded
- [ ] Media approval state is recorded
- [ ] Final approval is approved before deployment

## Paid Deployment Gate

- [ ] Paid media rights are approved if paid deployment is planned
- [ ] Rights dates are not expired
- [ ] Whitelisting is approved if the paid setup requires creator identity or account access
