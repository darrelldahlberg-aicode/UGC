# Creator Outreach Templates

## Initial Outreach

Hi [Creator Name],

I’m reaching out because your work around [specific category or content type] appears relevant to a UGC opportunity we’re evaluating for [brand or category, when approved to disclose].

We’re exploring [deliverable type] focused on [product/service or campaign job] with a target timing of [timeline]. The potential scope involves [creator posting / brand-owned UGC / paid usage / combination].

Are you interested in hearing the details? If so, please send your current availability, standard turnaround time, and the best contact for partnership details.

Thanks,
[Name]

## Follow-Up

Hi [Creator Name],

Following up on the UGC opportunity I sent on [date]. If you’re interested, I’ll send the scope and the few details we still need. If the timing is not a fit, a quick pass is helpful too.

Thanks,
[Name]

## Qualification Questions

Thanks for the interest. Before we finalize fit, please send:

- relevant sample work
- current availability and turnaround time
- any category conflicts or exclusivity limits
- your preferred deliverable formats
- any audience or performance information you want us to consider

Creator-provided performance information will be recorded as creator-provided until independently verified.

## Rate Request

Please send your current rate for the requested deliverables, including:

- base production rate
- included revision terms
- payment terms
- any separate cost for brand usage, paid media rights, or whitelisting/partnership-ad access

## Rights Clarification

Please confirm each item separately:

- creator-posted organic content: approved / not approved / not required
- brand-owned usage: approved / not approved / not required
- paid media usage: approved / not approved / not required
- whitelisting or partnership-ad access: approved / not approved / not required
- allowed channels
- rights start date
- rights end date
- renewal terms
- exclusivity terms, if any

## Decline / Closeout

Hi [Creator Name],

Thank you for sharing your information. We’re not moving forward with this specific opportunity, but we appreciate the time and will keep the fit in mind for future work where appropriate.

Thanks,
[Name]
