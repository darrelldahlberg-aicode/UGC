# UGC Media Kit / Partnership Pitch Template

Use this neutral template when preparing a creator-side or agency-side UGC partnership pitch. Reference examples may inform presentation structure, but they do not set scoring, pricing, or qualification rules.

## Creator Identity

- Name or creator brand
- Role or positioning statement
- Location
- Contact details
- Primary profile links

## Positioning

- Short value statement
- Audience or buyer relevance
- Creator strengths
- Brand categories served

## Audience and Platforms

For each platform:

- platform
- handle
- audience size, when supported by current evidence
- audience notes
- engagement or reach evidence, when available

## Content Categories

- category 1
- category 2
- category 3
- category 4
- category 5

## Services

Possible services may include:

- brand-owned UGC
- creator-posted content
- short-form video
- product demonstrations
- testimonials or experience-led content
- affiliate partnerships
- longer-term creator partnerships

Only list services actually offered.

## Selected Proof

For each proof point:

- campaign or content example
- objective
- result
- evidence source
- evidence state

## Analytics

Present only supported metrics. Examples:

- recent views or reach
- watch-time or completion metrics
- engagement metrics
- click or conversion metrics
- attributable revenue when independently supported or clearly labeled as creator-provided

## Partnership Options

- one-time deliverables
- multi-asset packages
- creator posting
- brand usage
- paid media availability
- whitelisting or partnership-ad availability
- long-term collaboration availability

## Prior Brand Work

- brand or category
- type of work
- public example or supporting source, when available

## Rights Availability

State separately:

- organic posting availability
- brand usage availability
- paid media availability
- whitelisting or partnership-ad availability
- typical rights term, if established

## Contact

- name
- email
- preferred contact channel
- profile links
