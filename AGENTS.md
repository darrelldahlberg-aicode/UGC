# Agent Rules for UGC

Read `SKILL.md` before acting on creator or UGC work.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- Do not invent creator metrics, rights, rates, approvals, or verification status.
- Preserve `creator_provided`, `agency_observed`, `verified`, and `unknown` as distinct evidence states.
- Never infer paid-media rights from organic posting rights.
- Never deploy content with expired or missing required rights.
- Never use files under `examples/` as scoring standards.
- Route specialist work to the owning repository instead of copying its logic here.
- Do not convert creator-provided claims into verified facts without independent evidence.
- Missing performance metrics remain unknown and must not be estimated.
- Final deployment requires the approvals and rights required by the deployment destination.

## Ownership Boundaries

Route work as follows:

- Content concepts, hooks, scripts, captions, and platform variants: Adley
- Broader campaign or brand strategy: Creative Operating System
- Video production, assembly, QA, and video delivery: AI Video
- Scaled publishing and content matrices: Content Scaling
- Workflow routing, retries, audit records, and orchestration: Otto

UGC owns the creator-specific operating rules and data contracts used in those handoffs.

## Reference Material

Files under `examples/` are examples only. They may inform presentation ideas. They must not become hidden scoring logic, rate anchors, qualification thresholds, or campaign requirements.
